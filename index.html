About the Conference

IEEE IGNITE 2.0 - IEEE aims to provide a vibrant platform for young researchers, innovators, and aspiring professionals from diverse disciplines including Engineering, Management, Pharmacy, Biosciences, Applied Sciences, Computer Applications, and other academic domains to present their ideas, showcase research contributions, and engage in meaningful and academic discussions with academicians, industry experts, researchers, and IEEE professionals.

By encouraging interdisciplinary learning and collaboration, the conference seeks to bridge the gap between academic knowledge and real-world technological, scientific, and industrial advancements. The conference will bring together participants from various IEEE Student Branch Chapters (SBCs) across IEEE Region 10 under the IEEE Madhya Pradesh Section, thereby creating a broader platform for technical interaction, networking, collaboration, and dissemination of innovative research work among students and young professionals.

IEEE IGNITE 2.0 will feature keynote and expert sessions delivered by eminent speakers on emerging technologies, innovation, entrepreneurship, critical thinking, and professional career development. In addition, dedicated Student-to-Young Professional interaction sessions will provide valuable insights into professional growth, industry expectations, research pathways, and the transition from student life to successful professional careers.

IEEE IGNITE 2.0 will also include multidisciplinary undergraduate and postgraduate technical paper presentation tracks, enabling participants to strengthen their research aptitude, technical communication, critical thinking, and presentation skills while receiving constructive feedback from experts and peers. Outstanding research presentations and innovative contributions will be recognized through awards and honors, motivating students toward academic and professional excellence. Funded under the IEEE SPAx Program and supported by the IEEE Foundation, the conference aspires to create an intellectually enriching and collaborative environment that empowers students to innovate, connect, and contribute meaningfully to the advancement of science, technology, healthcare, management, and society.
